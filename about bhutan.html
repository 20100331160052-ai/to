About Bhutan

Bhutan is a small country located in the eastern Himalayas. It is known for its beautiful mountains, green forests, peaceful valleys and rich culture.

The capital city of Bhutan is Thimphu. Bhutan has many traditional buildings, monasteries and historical places.

Quick Facts

  • Capital: Thimphu
  • Region: Himalayas
  • Currency: Ngultrum
  • Language: Dzongkha
  • Famous for: Gross National Happiness

Culture

Bhutan has a rich culture and many traditional customs and festivals.

Natural Beauty

Bhutan has high mountains, rivers, forests and beautiful valleys. Nature is an important part of Bhutanese life.
